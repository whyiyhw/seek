### Pinning the autopilot lane is not enough — its control flow was hard-wired to the metered client
- **Saw**: the c_kdblc4 env contract pins cron-fired `seek autopilot run` to the flat-rate opencode lane, yet the pinned child failed with "autopilot requires a model provider" (valid OpenCode key present).
- **Why**: `runAutopilot` and the decomposer accepted only `*deepseek.Client` (`dsClient` is nil on second-tier lanes); nothing adapted `llm.Provider` to the verifier's `JudgeClient`, and the DeepSeek lane is the metered one.
- **Fix**: decomposer narrowed to `autopilot.DecomposeClient` (= `verifier.JudgeClient` shape) plus `llmChatClient` (one `ChatStream` → single-shot Chat); `runAutopilot` takes the client-or-provider union. Rework: the adapter built `llm.ChatRequest` without `SessionID`, and openai sets `x-opencode-session` only when non-empty — production got `400 MissingSessionID` on first decompose while the e2e fake (no header check) stayed green; now `runAutopilot` mints one `session.EphemeralID()` per run, forwarded on every `Chat` (decompose+judge share one id; workers keep per-spawn sub-sids), and the fake returns 400 without the header.
- **Lesson**: a lane-pinning billing gate must be paired with proof the workload runs on that lane; an interface-shaped adapter inherits every field the real wire contract requires, and a sandbox that doesn't enforce the remote's mandatory header proves only its invented happy path.
