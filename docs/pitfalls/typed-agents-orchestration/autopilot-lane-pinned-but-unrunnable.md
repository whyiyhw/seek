### Pinning the autopilot lane is not enough — its control flow was hard-wired to the metered client
- **Saw**: c_kdblc4's env contract pins cron-fired `seek autopilot run` to the flat-rate opencode lane, but the child failed "autopilot requires a model provider" (valid OpenCode key present).
- **Why**: `runAutopilot` and the decomposer took only `*deepseek.Client` (`dsClient` nil on second-tier lanes); nothing adapted `llm.Provider` to the verifier's `JudgeClient`; DeepSeek is the metered lane.
- **Fix**: decomposer narrowed to `autopilot.DecomposeClient` (= `verifier.JudgeClient` shape) + `llmChatClient` (one `ChatStream` → single-shot Chat); `runAutopilot` takes the client-or-provider union. Rework: the adapter built `llm.ChatRequest` without `SessionID` while openai sets `x-opencode-session` only when non-empty → prod `400 MissingSessionID`, fake (no header check) green; now one `session.EphemeralID()` per run on every `Chat` (decompose+judge share it, workers keep per-spawn sub-sids), and the fake 400s without the header.
- **Lesson**: a lane-pinning billing gate needs proof the workload runs on it; an interface-shaped adapter inherits every field the wire contract requires, and a sandbox without the remote's mandatory header proves only the invented happy path.
