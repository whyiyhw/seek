### A panic-recover defer must sit INSIDE the stop observer — LIFO decides who reports the outcome
- **Saw**: adding panic containment to `PromptFiles` after `main` added a `NotifyStop` observer: the obvious placement (recover right after `defer close(out)`) reports the wrong outcome — the observer sees `Finish="max_turns"` (zero-ish default) for a panic death.
- **Why**: defers are LIFO — the observer rule ("terminal observer is the LAST defer, runs before `close(out)`") pins only its relation to the channel close; anything registered after the observer runs before it, which a recover needs to mutate what the observer reads.
- **Fix**: the recover defer is registered AFTER the Stop observer, before the `currentEvents` reset; it sets `stopFinish = "error"` then emits the `ErrorEvent`, so the observer fires with the honest finish; the LIFO order is recorded as contract (c_vfgeam).
- **Lesson**: defer order in `PromptFiles` is load-bearing: `currentEvents` cleared → panic recover → Stop observer → `close(out)`; observing defers last, mutating defers right after the observer, both before close.
