### A panic-recover defer must sit INSIDE the stop observer — LIFO decides who reports the outcome
- **Saw**: giving `PromptFiles` panic containment (it has a `NotifyStop` observer added on `main`): recovering right after `defer close(out)` reports the wrong outcome — the observer sees `Finish="max_turns"` (zero-ish default) on panic.
- **Why**: defers are LIFO — the observer rule ("terminal observer is the LAST defer, runs before `close(out)`") pins only its relation to the channel close; anything registered after the observer runs before it, and only there can a recover mutate what the observer reads.
- **Fix**: the recover defer is registered AFTER the Stop observer, before the `currentEvents` reset: it sets `stopFinish = "error"` then emits the `ErrorEvent`, so the observer fires with the honest finish; LIFO order is contract (c_vfgeam).
- **Lesson**: defer order in `PromptFiles` is load-bearing: `currentEvents` cleared → panic recover → Stop observer → `close(out)`; observing defers last, mutating ones right after the observer, both before close.
