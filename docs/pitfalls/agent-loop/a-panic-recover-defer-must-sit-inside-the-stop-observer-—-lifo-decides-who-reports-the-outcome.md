### A panic-recover defer must sit INSIDE the stop observer — LIFO decides who reports the outcome

- **Saw**: adding panic containment to `PromptFiles` while `main` had meanwhile added a `NotifyStop` observer produced a merge in which the obvious placement (recover registered first, right after `defer close(out)`) silently reports the wrong outcome twice: the observer sees `Finish="max_turns"` (the zero-ish default) for a turn that actually died of a panic, because the recover ran *after* it.
- **Why**: Go defers are LIFO. The observer's rule ("terminal observer is the LAST defer registered, so it runs before `close(out)`") only pins its relation to the channel close — not to other defers. Anything registered *after* the observer runs *before* it, which is exactly what a recover needs if it is to mutate the value the observer reads.
- **Fix**: the recover defer is registered AFTER the Stop observer and before the `currentEvents` reset; it sets `stopFinish = "error"` and then emits the `ErrorEvent`, so the observer still fires with the honest finish and both sends land before `close(out)`. Comment in place records the LIFO order as a contract (c_vfgeam).
- **Lesson**: in `PromptFiles` the defer order is load-bearing and now three-deep — `currentEvents` cleared → panic recover → Stop observer → `close(out)`. When adding a defer that *observes* (Stop) put it last; when adding one that *mutates what an observer reads* (recover), put it immediately after the observer. Both land before the channel close, which is the property callers actually depend on.
